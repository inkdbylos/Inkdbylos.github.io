Prepare

Before your appointment

  1. Confirm. You’ll get a confirmation text at least the day before — reply to confirm.
  2. Eat first. Have a proper meal and bring water.
  3. Arrive clean. Shower; keep the area free of lotions, oils or heavy products.
  4. Dress for access. Comfortable clothes that won’t interfere with the area.
  5. Bring ID and anything for a long session — headphones, water, a snack.
Heal

Aftercare

  1. Wash with unscented antibacterial soap and pat dry.
  2. Apply a thin layer of healing ointment.
  3. Avoid direct sun, swimming and saunas.
  4. Don’t pick. Let it heal naturally.
  5. Stay hydrated and wear loose clothing to reduce irritation.

Typical healing time: 2–4 weeks.

Questions

FAQ

Something not covered here? Text 918-212-8144.

How is my price decided?

After I review your idea, size, placement and level of detail, I’ll reach out with your quote and how many sessions the piece needs — before you book anything.

Can I book every session for a large piece at once?

Yes. Your booking link lets you reserve all of your sessions in one go. Sessions are spaced at least 3 weeks apart so each stage can heal.

Do you take smaller tattoos?

Yes. Smaller pieces are booked as custom-length weekday evening sessions.

When are appointments available?

Full days on Saturdays and Sundays, and weekday evenings from 5:30 PM. Your private booking link shows the real open times for your session type, up to 3 months ahead.

How do I pay my deposit?

By Zelle or Cash App. No payments are taken on this website — after you choose your time, you’ll see both options and have 2 hours to send your deposit.

Will I see my design before the appointment?

Designs are created for confirmed appointments, after your deposit is received. We can discuss and adjust the direction before your session.
